NOTE FOR THE DEMO: This pathology report documents the diagnosis (invasive ductal
carcinoma), the clinical stage (T2 N1 M0, IIB), and ER/PR receptor status, but the
HER2 result is EQUIVOCAL (IHC 2+) with reflex ISH still pending, so a definitive
HER2 status is NOT on file. HER2 is the load-bearing data element for this regimen:
the ordered TH (paclitaxel + trastuzumab) is the accepted adjuvant regimen only for
HER2-POSITIVE disease. The readiness step (Step 1.5) must detect that HER2 is not
yet resolved and collect it before prior authorization can proceed (the DTR /
needs-more-info path). Once HER2 resolves POSITIVE, TH for adjuvant HER2+ breast
cancer is NCCN Category 1 (medically accepted under UM-9) and is APPROVED; were HER2
NEGATIVE, TH would not be the accepted adjuvant regimen and would be DENIED.
